# ChainProof Competitive Positioning

## Category

ChainProof is a **metric trust and reconciliation platform for AI analytics**.

## Core message

> One KPI name. Three valid calculations. One governed enterprise answer.

## USP

> Semantic layers govern agreed metrics. ChainProof governs the disagreement that exists before agreement.

## Comparison

| Category | Typical capability | ChainProof addition |
|---|---|---|
| BI dashboard | display configured KPIs | detect and explain incompatible definitions |
| semantic layer | publish agreed business definitions | manage conflict, human approval, versioning, and activation before publication |
| data catalog | document names, owners, and lineage | execute formulas and compare actual result impact |
| data-quality platform | identify malformed or incomplete data | identify valid data producing incompatible business meanings |
| generic AI copilot | translate questions into SQL | refuse ambiguity until a governed metric and scope are available |
| metric store | centralize metric logic | preserve conflict, approval, activation, rollback, and evidence history |

## Why the topic is competitive

The supply-chain domain itself is not unique. The competitive edge is the narrow problem:

```text
correct data
+
correct SQL
+
ambiguous business definition
=
confidently wrong AI answer
```

ChainProof solves that gap.

## Rubric alignment

### Real-world relevance

- cross-functional KPI disputes are common;
- supplier accountability depends on date and quality rules;
- conflicting management reports create operational and commercial risk;
- Data Steward governance is a recognizable enterprise role.

### Technical execution

- RAW, CORE, GOVERNANCE, SEMANTIC, APP, and AUDIT separation;
- native Semantic View and verified questions;
- Cortex Analyst with scope and read-only SQL controls;
- evidence-backed review;
- version activation and rollback history;
- fail-fast certification and limitation recording.

### Solution completeness

- deployed Streamlit product;
- complete pre-approval to trusted-answer journey;
- calculation and policy evidence;
- documentation, presentation, screenshots, and video script;
- production-hardening and recovery plan.

## What not to add before submission

Do not dilute the story with:

- demand forecasting;
- route optimization;
- a second external frontend;
- blockchain;
- generic multi-agent dashboards;
- dozens of unrelated KPIs;
- ungoverned approval writes;
- unsupported claims of production RBAC.

## Closing position

ChainProof is not trying to predict the next supply-chain event. It makes sure everyone agrees on what the current metric means before AI acts on it.
